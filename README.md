      <h2>Goal and objective</h2>
      <p>       
        Scanner365 is an application developed to help businesses accomodate for the amount of stock they have without the use of a stock machine.
        The aim of this scanner is to enable businesses to scan products easily and use the information obtained to keep track of their stock without having to manually scan and punch in the quantity of each product.
      </p>
      <h2>Why is this important?</h2>
      <p>
        Keeping track of stock plays an integral part in improving business growth and managing supply. However some businesses still use older technology to keep track of their stock. 
        This is a problem as the more we progress into the 4th Industrial revolution the greater the gap between older and newer technology becomes. For instance, if I wanted to scan a product I would need to use a stock scanner
        to keep track of the supply and make sure that the quantities are right. This becomes problematic as the process is long and the machine is complicated to use. As businesses grow their work force grows as well, which means more people will need to be shown
        how the machine works. Unfortunately not every worker is capable of understanding the intricacies of these machines, thus leading to poor performance and unreliable management of supply/stock. To make sure everyworker is capable of understanding how to record stock
        they need to have access to a machine (or app) that will allow them to do the same job with less effort.
      </p>  
      <h2>How does it work</h2>
      <p>
        Using the camera on mobile phones, this scanner scans the barcodes of products and adds them to a list. Once added, the user must enter in the quantity of the product. The product can occur multiple times in the page and 
        will also specify the time at which the scanning took place. 
        Then there is an option to download the file as a text file which is structured as such:
        <br><br>
        (Barcode),(quantity)
        <br><br>
        Though simple, it will allow users to enter the data more easily into the table.
        They also have an option to clear the list and restart with a clean slate.
        <br><br>
        There is also an option to upload media files onto the application should they need the scanner to keep track of items that are not recored yet but are not currently present. This can help workers communicate with their superiors more efficiently should there be any issues
        pertaining to the stock they captured.
      </p>
      <h2>Conclusion</h2>
      <p>With the new Scanner 365 companies will be able to keep track of their stock easily and increase/improve their work force.</p>
